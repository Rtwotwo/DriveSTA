Beyond a Single Human Demonstration:
Learning from Scored Trajectory Alternatives for End-to-End Driving

Xinyao Rao1 Zecheng Tang2 Liuxuan Sun1 Zheyuan Lai3 Minjun Yu3 Xun Cao1 Wei Li1,3,*

DriveSTA demosNAVSIM · HUGSIM
2560 × 1440 · Multi-scene driving demonstrationsDownload MP4 ↓

Driving logs record one executed trajectory, while a planner must generate and compare many feasible alternatives. DriveSTA turns these records into scored trajectory supervision: constrained path construction and screening yield 2.61M high-quality targets from 103.3k real scenes. Dense matching teaches distinct proposals, and replay of successful and failed paths trains their scores. A second stage learns local residual corrections, guided by a pretrained Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA) video encoder adapted to driving observations. DriveSTA-Scale reaches 95.55 NAVTEST PDMS; its Proposal planner scores 91.32 NAVTEST EPDMS and 56.13 NAVHARD EPDMS. On 345 HUGSIM scenarios, Refinement achieves 56.09 route completion and 46.69 HD-Score.

Selection rule and full-set comparison

All 41 qualifying scenes are included: DriveSTA PDMS ≥ 95 and each of the three baselines < 80. Thirteen have zero scores for all three baselines. These are selected qualitative cases, drawn from a full 12.1k-scene evaluation with no scoring failures.
